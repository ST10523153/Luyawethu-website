About us

Ours, as in everyone's.

Luyawethu started as a handful of shared houses run by people who remembered what first-year accommodation used to feel like — and decided to fix it.

Mission

Housing that doesn't get in the way of your degree.

We handle security, maintenance, and the admin headaches — so a bad landlord, a broken geyser, or a noisy corridor never becomes the reason a semester goes sideways.

Vision

A residence students choose to stay in.

Not just a bed near campus — a place people recommend to the next year's intake, because the community and the upkeep are worth staying for.

History

How we got here

2014

Three houses, one waiting list

Luyawethu opened with three converted houses near campus and a waiting list that told us there was real demand for something better run.

2018

The first purpose-built block

We moved from converted houses into our first residence built specifically for student living — proper study floors, shared kitchens, and secure access from day one.

2022

A residence committee, formalised

Residents started running their own events and raising maintenance issues directly — so we built a standing committee to make that official.

Today

Four room types, one community

From single ensuites to shared quads, still run on the same idea: make it easy to live well while you study.
