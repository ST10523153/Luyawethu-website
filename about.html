About Us

Discover the story behind Luyawethu Student Residences

Our History

Luyawethu Student Residences is a small, privately-owned student accommodation provider located in close proximity to Vaal University. The business was established to address the growing need for safe, affordable, and convenient housing options for university students in the area.

With only four single rooms available — one with a private ensuite bathroom — Luyawethu offers a personalised and homely living environment, distinguishing itself from larger, impersonal student housing complexes. The name "Luyawethu" means "it's ours" — reflecting the vision of creating a welcoming and supportive home away from home for students.

Our Mission

To provide secure, comfortable, and affordable student accommodation that fosters academic success and personal well-being.

Our Vision

To become the preferred student residence choice for Vaal University students by consistently delivering exceptional living experiences and building a strong community of academic excellence.

Our Core Values

  • Safety: A secure environment where students can focus on their studies.
  • Community: Building a supportive and inclusive living community.
  • Excellence: Providing high-quality accommodation and services.
  • Integrity: Transparent and honest communication with students and parents.

Why Choose Luyawethu?

  • 🏠 Safe and Secure: 24/7 security with access control and CCTV.
  • 📍 Prime Location: Walking distance to Vaal University.
  • 🛏️ Four Single Rooms: One with a private ensuite bathroom.
  • 🍳 Shared Kitchen: Fully equipped with stove, oven, microwave, and fridge.
  • 🧺 Laundry Room: Washing machine and dryer on-site.
  • 📶 Fibre Wi-Fi: High-speed internet in every room.
  • 🔐 Secure Parking: Safe parking available for residents.
